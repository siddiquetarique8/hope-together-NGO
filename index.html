Together for a Better Tomorrow

We started in 2016 with three volunteers and one classroom. Today we run programs across four states.

Our Story

From one blackboard to hundreds of classrooms

HopeTogether began when three friends started weekend classes for children in a Gurugram settlement whose school had shut down. A blackboard propped against a wall became a structured after-school programme within a year.

Today we are a team of 40+ staff and several hundred volunteers working across education, healthcare, community development and environmental care. Our approach has barely changed since that first classroom: show up consistently, listen first, and measure what we actually deliver.

Join our journey
Our Values

What guides every decision

Dignity first

We work with people, never on them. Every program begins by asking what is actually needed.

Transparency

Public annual reports, audited accounts and clear spending breakdowns for every program.

Consistency

Long-term presence beats one-off drives. We commit to communities for years, not weeks.

Local leadership

The best person to run a program is usually someone who already lives there.

Key Focus Areas

Four pillars guide everything we do — each chosen because it removes a real barrier between a family and a fair chance.

Education

Every child deserves a classroom

Scholarships, school support and learning resources for children who would otherwise go without.

  • Scholarships covering fees, uniforms and books
  • After-school learning centres with trained local teachers
  • Digital literacy sessions for older students
  • Bridge courses for children returning to school

Healthcare

Care that comes to the community

Medical camps, basic healthcare and awareness programs reaching families with no clinic nearby.

  • Monthly camps with visiting doctors and free diagnostics
  • Mother and child health check-ups
  • Seasonal vaccination and awareness drives
  • Referral support for hospital care

FAQ

Questions people often ask

How much of my donation reaches the programs?

About 84 paise of every rupee goes directly to programs. The rest covers audits, compliance and the small core team that keeps everything running.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. HopeTogether is registered under Section 12A and donations qualify for deduction under Section 80G. A receipt is emailed right after your gift.

How long is the volunteer commitment?

We ask for a minimum of three months so the children and families you work with get consistency. Orientation is provided before your first session.

Can my company partner with you?

Absolutely. We work with companies on CSR programs, employee volunteering and in-kind donations. Use the contact page to start the conversation.
